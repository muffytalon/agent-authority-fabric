# Gate 1 sabotage report

Generated by `compose/sabotage.py` on 2026-11-13.

**28 of 28 C-ENG and C-OUT attacks were seen to fail with a defence removed.** The baseline, on the same build with every switch off, held 28 of 28. 35 of the 35 sabotage results were caught.

The sabotage is in a temporary copy of the tree, under its own image tag. An attack is **caught** if the oracle saw a bypass or the attempt succeeded. **Refused elsewhere** means another guard stopped the call, so the attack never got past, and it does not count. **Survived** means it held with the defence gone.
